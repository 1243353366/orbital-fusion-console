Orbital Fusion Console is an independent open-source prototype. It is not endorsed by NASA, ESA, the European Union, OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles, OpenStreetMap, MITRE, CISA, or any commercial imagery provider named in its connector catalog.

## Map and Earth-observation data

NASA Earth-observation browse imagery is requested at runtime from **NASA EOSDIS Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS)**. NASA should be acknowledged as the source. Dataset-specific restrictions, if any, take precedence over general NASA guidance.

The graceful map fallback uses NASA Scientific Visualization Studio’s **Earth at Night 2012** visualization, downscaled and re-encoded without semantic alteration. Source: <https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/30028>.

Basemap context is served by **OpenFreeMap** using **OpenMapTiles** and **OpenStreetMap** data. Required attribution is added automatically by MapLibre and must not be removed by downstream deployments.

NASA HLS metadata references the **NASA Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2 Project**. HLS object access may require Earthdata credentials, and its registry listing identifies a CC BY 4.0 license.

Copernicus and commercial-provider names appear only as connector-policy examples. No commercial imagery is bundled, scraped, or redistributed.
